# Memphis — Product roadmap

This is a direction, not a release schedule. The phases describe what we want to prove next; existing prototype pages do not mean those outcomes have been validated.

## 1. Make one song worth exploring

Create a small, carefully researched collection with approachable explanations of sound and creation, relevant credits, sources and links to external listening. Choose depth and clarity before catalog size.

**Move forward when:** a curious listener can learn something, notice it while listening and understand where the information came from.

## 2. Turn individual discoveries into connected journeys

Connect songs, collaborators, techniques, genres and stories. Offer short guided paths and explain why each next step belongs.

**Move forward when:** people can follow their curiosity without needing to know what to search for next.

## 3. Give listeners a place of their own

Help people save discoveries, organize collections, keep notes and choose interests. Use the existing journal where it supports these needs. Establish clear control over personal data before expanding personalization.

**Move forward when:** people return to continue their own exploration and can manage what Memphis remembers.

## 4. Make discovery and reflection more personal

Introduce recommendations with visible reasons and adjustable preferences. Explore richer listening recaps only where users choose to provide sufficient data. Show coverage and uncertainty, and leave room for discovery beyond familiar tastes.

**Move forward when:** listeners find the suggestions useful and can understand and correct the assumptions behind them.

## 5. Connect understanding to creation

Add approachable comparisons, listening exercises and small creative experiments. Help people explore how performance, rhythm, harmony and arrangement change what they hear.

**Move forward when:** a listener can use something they learned to make or reinterpret a small musical idea.

## Throughout

Maintain source quality, respect permissions for content and data, and distinguish evidence from interpretation. Preserve the original interface as the Design Archive. Let listener feedback determine how far each phase grows.

## Keep outside the first release

A full streaming catalog, a universal explanation for hit songs, advanced recommendation models, exhaustive listening statistics and a complete music production environment.

## Next decision

Use the [product core hypothesis](product-core.md) to test a repeatable song experience combining attention over time, musical explanation and personal connections. Verify one viable trend data source before committing to tracking features.

Choose a small set of songs and the questions each can help answer. Build and review one complete discovery journey before expanding the collection.

The [vision](direction.md) explains the purpose. The [implementation roadmap](roadmap.md) records the earlier technical phases and reported progress; its completed checkboxes are not a measure of product validation.
